# Community standards

We want project discussions to stay useful, respectful and focused on the work.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive, including when reviewing code or disagreeing about design.
- Explain criticism with evidence and focus on the work, not personal traits.
- Respect privacy. Do not share credentials, private customer details or real financial records.
- Follow the repository's security reporting process for vulnerabilities instead of posting exploit details publicly.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks and sharing private information without consent.

If a discussion becomes abusive or unsafe, contact the project maintainers privately with relevant context. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community and project.
